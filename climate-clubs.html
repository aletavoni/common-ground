COMMON GROUNDCooperation is one outcome. Welfare is another.

A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

How the game works

You control one nation; two bots control the others. Every round begins with a fresh 12-point endowment for each nation. Earnings accumulate, but do not change next round’s budget. The original experiment used 30 rounds; the five-round option is a classroom shortcut.

Keep or contribute

Keep all 12 points for yourself, or contribute all 12 to the public account. A Small contribution generates 4 points for each nation, Medium generates 6 for each, and Large generates 8 for each. This benefit goes to contributors and noncontributors alike.

Before sanctions, your earnings equal 12 if you keep, plus the sum of public benefits. If all contribute, everyone earns 18. If everyone keeps, everyone earns 12. If only Medium and Large contribute, the earnings are Small 26, Medium 14 and Large 14.

Five sets of rules

  • Public goods baseline: keep or contribute; no sanctions.
  • I will if you will: adds a conditional contribution. It activates if another nation contributes unconditionally or also conditionally commits. A lone conditional contributor keeps the 12 points instead.
  • Public goods + sanctions: cooperators can commit to sanctioning all noncontributors. Noncontributors can commit to retaliating against everyone who sanctions them.
  • Conditional + sanctions: adds conditional cooperation and allows the sanction decision separately. A failed conditional contribution does not impose sanctions or retaliate.
  • Climate club: a treaty commits you to contribute and sanction noncontributors if at least one other nation chooses a treaty or unconditionally contributes and commits to sanctions. An unconditional contributor without sanctions does not activate a lone treaty. A failed treaty keeps its 12 points and does not sanction or retaliate. Ordinary unconditional choices remain available.

Sanctions and retaliation

Each actual sanction costs its sender 1 point per target and removes 8 from Small, 5 from Medium or 2 from Large. Multiple sanctions add up. Sanctions target every actual noncontributor; you cannot select one particular target.

If you kept your points and chose retaliation, you retaliate only against the nations that actually sanctioned you. Each retaliation costs you 1 and removes 8, 5 or 2 depending on the target’s size. There is one retaliation step, with no further escalation. A fine is destroyed, not transferred to the sender. Net earnings can be negative.

All choices are committed before the round’s outcomes are revealed. You cannot wait to see another nation’s action and then decide whether to sanction. The single form combines the experiment’s contribution and response choices without adding information between them.

What the bots mean

The selected profile specifies a strategy for each of Small, Medium and Large. You replace one of those players and can deviate. Each preset has been checked as a pure-strategy Nash equilibrium of the one-round game: no player can earn more by changing their strategy alone while the others hold theirs fixed. Some best responses tie.

Bots repeat those same complete strategies, including how conditional choices and sanctions respond to your current commitment. They do not learn from your history. Deviating need not produce another equilibrium. The after-round best-response comparison values only your own points, not fairness or group welfare.

Why more cooperation need not mean more earnings

Sanctions and retaliation burn points for both senders and targets. A threat can support cooperation without being used, but actual sanctions reduce surplus. Also, contributor identity matters: Medium and Large cooperating alone can generate the same group gross earnings of 54 as all three cooperating, while distributing those earnings differently.

Research and implementation notes

Based on Casari & Tavoni (2024), Climate clubs in the laboratory, Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Economics 110, 102211, and the original experiment instructions.

The implemented 4/6/8 public returns follow the payoff equation, numerical examples and original instructions. A nearby paragraph gives different decimal marginal returns; those decimals are not used. Treaty activation follows the original instructions, including the recognition of an unconditional cooperator who commits to sanctions. Bot presets are verified directly under these rules.

The app keeps the three sizes, endowments, all five strategy sets, deterministic point payoffs and simultaneous information structure. Teaching additions include the optional shorter session, choice of nation and bot profile, upfront disclosure of the bot strategies and a detailed payoff ledger. The study’s neutral participant framing is replaced with fictional nations. There are no real sanctions, payments, data collection or guarantees of experimental earnings.

Everything runs locally in your browser. A refresh resets play. CSV downloads are created on your device. The app has no analytics or game backend; a website host may maintain ordinary access logs.
